About Troport

Troport Company Limited was incorporated on 12 November 2025 as a private limited company under the Companies Act, 2015. It is owned and run by two Kenyan directors who each hold half of the company's shares.

On 4 December 2025 the company was registered under the Access to Government Procurement Opportunities (AGPO) programme in the Youth category. This allows Troport to bid for public tenders reserved for youth-owned businesses, and it is also registered with the Kenya Revenue Authority for company income tax.

What we do

Troport supplies goods and services to public and private buyers, with the paperwork and compliance to back every bid.

Public-sector supply

We bid for and deliver government tenders reserved for youth-owned businesses under AGPO, in line with the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015.

Private-sector supply

We provide goods and services to businesses and organisations that need a registered, tax-compliant supplier they can invoice and rely on.

Compliant by default

Incorporated, registered with KRA and certified under AGPO, so buyers can verify who we are before they work with us.
